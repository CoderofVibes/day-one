## Limits and copy to correct

The contract checks the model’s signed response against the supplied hash and relies on the caller to construct it from the PO. Describe the checked fields and the supplied fingerprint. The recorded retry is a positive example. A claim that every argument is independently bound would need a stronger check and a negative test. [Approval and funding checks](../../contracts/spend-policy/src/lib.rs#L595), [decision check](../../contracts/spend-policy/src/lib.rs#L1504).

Count-based settlement needs ordered and received quantities, and the invoice caps payment when recorded. Pooled orders pay in full on release. The USDC run uses the earlier contract. Say explicitly that staff report receipt and QC. [Settlement](../../contracts/spend-policy/src/lib.rs#L1472), [pooled record](../../contracts/spend-policy/src/lib.rs#L1193).
- **[Lines 195 and 266](../../README.md#L195), “Above $2,500 a PO must carry” a signed decision.** Scope it to the configured Plant B policy and the plain-PO path. [Threshold check](../../contracts/spend-policy/src/lib.rs#L575), [attestation code](../../contracts/spend-policy/src/attest.rs).
- **[Lines 9, 202 and 229](../../README.md#L9), “recorded attacks, each stopped,” “each one refused,” and “Kevin is not fooled.”** Say the replay suite stopped its 21 scenarios and name the guard that did it. One split order funded before the cap blocked the second. In the short-delivery case the authorized release succeeds at a reduced amount. Mail and ERP holds happen off chain. [Attack replay](../../scripts/attacks.ts), [split-purchase proof](../../PROOF.md#attacks-on-kevin-and-the-erp-path).
- **[Line 276](../../README.md#L276), “Only the Nov 9–16 run is recorded on testnet.”** Those are the main demo’s fictional dates. Other flows also have recorded transactions. The lot-book history is generated, and its roots were batch-anchored. [Proof-log contents](../../PROOF.md#contents).
- **[Lines 75 and 274](../../README.md#L75), dated test count and faucet rate.** The README lists 985 tests in 131 files. This checkout collected 989 in 132 files. Point to `npm run check` and CI for the count. The faucet rate is unnecessary to show what was paid. [CI](../../.github/workflows/ci.yml).
