## The five worst problems

1. **Anything the recording did not do fails silently, with a developer error somewhere else.** "Decline PO-1042" leaves the desk exactly as it was; about a screen further down, in the "Up next" panel, a red box prints `replay call #7 differs from the recording: expected {"args":{"amount":"5150000000",…` and runs off its right edge. A wrong answer to Kevin's question turns green, enables Confirm, and then Confirm does nothing where you clicked. (Findings 23, 26, 27, 31.)
2. **There are two desks and nothing says which one you are on.** Opening `/` gives a working desk with three scanned records waiting. "Meet Kevin" → "Open my desk" (or "Start your shift") gives a different one: a recorded replay with Play, "Jump to" and "Step 1 of 15 · Load seed · ← Back · Next →", where the scan queue is gone. The README's own instructions ("Upload the batch record, then Review BR-B-1045") match neither label on the working desk. (Findings 2, 45.)
3. **Numbers disagree on the same screen.** 38, 63 and 14 recorded transactions in one view of "Every proof". Spend this month is $5,150 beside $612 "set aside", and on Spending limits it is $5,150, $10,962.00 and $14,995.00. The shortage is 514 kg in one panel and 101.7 kg in the next. Kevin says 167 kg of magnesium oxide; the page says 84 kg. PO-1042, the order that went over the limit, opens with "placed within the limits HQ set". (Findings 14, 29, 34, 35, 38, 39, 40, 41, 42.)
4. **The story's main moment is skipped on Ray's desk, and its ending reads as if it never happened.** Confirm BR-B-1045 and the next screen is Linda approving $5,150: the shortage, the three quotes and a first order all pass as lines in a side list. After the 700 kg arrives and is paid, the brief and Inventory still say "need about 824 kg. Recommend ordering 700 kg", the same figures as before. (Findings 25, 36.)
5. **The judge panel covers what it points at.** It takes the bottom third of the window: over Approve and Decline and the refused entry on step 3, and over the "Prove it" button on step 6, whose caption says "Press Prove it". On step 1, Next changes the page but the panel still says "Step 1 of 6" with the same words, and the dimmed page underneath still takes clicks. (Findings 8, 9, 10.)

## What worked well

- **"Nothing posts until you confirm", and then it says exactly what changed.** After each confirm the card reads "Inventory updated: 8 materials taken off stock…", with the before and after figures. Keep that sentence.
- **Linda's approval card.** The limit bar ($2,500 against $5,150), "the money is set aside, not paid", and the banner "the replay now plays Linda Park's part: this decision and its buttons are Linda's, not Ray's" were all clear on the first read.
- **The morning review's three lines per draft:** "Says", "Kevin", "Signing does", then one Sign button and "Ray: BR-B-1049 moved from Nov 19 to Nov 12." The refused bank-details email sitting in the same list is the best argument on the page.
- **The spreadsheet wizard, the recall drill and "Prove it".** Nine questions in plain words with Kevin's proposed answer for each; "Traced in 2.1 ms · by hand: about 2 days of paper"; "3 of 3 records proved" with a button to break one. A newcomer understands each in one click.
- **The phone.** No sideways scroll on any of the 22 pages at 390 px; the scan is readable there; the menu is clean in dark. The honest labels ("fictional", "Linda's text is simulated and the server holds her key", "a demo ERP, not a live integration") earned trust every time they appeared.
