# First-use review, Nov 5 2026

Cold walkthrough of the built web UI behind `npm run api` (keyless replay) in the desktop app's browser pane, at 1280×800 and 390×844, light and dark. Two personas: a hackathon judge with five minutes who read the README's first screen, and Ray Delgado on his first morning. No source, docs or repo screenshots were read. Nothing was signed and no model was called. Screenshots are in `first-use/<n>.png`.

## Status (Nov 5, Claude, after the fixes)

All 54 findings were checked against the code before changing anything; 50 are fixed, 1 was skipped on Tony's word and 3 were addressed differently:

- **Skipped:** 16 (the desk stays dark in the light theme; Tony, Nov 5).
- **Addressed differently:** 23 (no text box: the server holds no owner key by design, so the proof is retitled "a recorded example" and the terminal command folds away); 41 (the dim closed card is the card's 260 ms fade-in frozen partway, as a hidden browser pane pauses animations; the colours are unchanged); 44 (twin label overprint: `npm run web:shot:3d` already counts overprinted labels and reports 0; not changed).
- **Root causes worth knowing:** 2, the blank twin, came from drei's `<Html>` labels unmounting their React roots inside a commit (a removeChild error in the production build), now `web/src/twin3d/ScreenLabel.tsx`; the pane being hidden also pauses drawing, and a lost WebGL canvas now says so with "Draw it again". 35, the wrong number, is not a stale recording: Talk to Kevin (recorded or live) reads the end-of-run records, so every answer now says so.
- Screenshots were moved out of the repo to `C:Devday-one-refsirst-use-2026-11-05` (17.7 MB).

## The five worst problems, in order

1. **The Plant twin page comes up blank.** Opening `?page=map` directly, and judge-mode stop 5 ("Received, released, paid"), show an empty beige box with three uncaught `removeChild` errors in the console. The twin only draws after clicking the sidebar link from another page. The 3D twin is a headline feature in the README and it is the one page a judge may never see. (findings 2, 34)
2. **Judge mode changes the page under you.** Stop 1 opens on the Spreadsheets page scrolled mid-table, and about five seconds later, with no click, it is the Overview and the clock has moved from 5:00 to 5:12 AM while the card still says "Step 1 of 6". From stop 3 there are two step counters and two "Next →" buttons on screen (the judge card's "Step 3 of 6" and the run's "STEP 8 OF 15"). (findings 10, 12, 20)
3. **Ray's screen turns into Linda's with one click.** After Ray confirms the last batch record, the desk card becomes "Approve PO-1042 / Decline PO-1042" with the buttons live. Clicking Approve approves as Linda, jumps the clock to Fri Nov 13 and hands the screen to Tommy. Nothing says "you are now Linda". The Up next box even shows Linda's phone instruction, "Reply YES 1042 or NO 1042", to Ray. (findings 27, 62)
4. **Talk to Kevin gives a number that contradicts the page it sits beside.** A typed question, "How much ascorbic acid do we have on hand?", came back "1,010 kg on hand" stamped "Signed in enclave ✓", while Inventory & forecast on the same clock says 310 kg. "Kevin is reading the records…" reads as live, but the answer is a recording from another moment. (finding 31)
5. **There is no front door.** With no parameters the app lands on Ray's desk mid-task. "Judge mode" is hidden in the ≡ menu, "Meet Kevin · first shift" only appears in that menu after you are already in judge mode, and the only line that says this is a replay is the page footer. The "Live" pill beside 7d/28d/90d/12m looks like a toggle, is not clickable, and is the wrong word for a keyless replay. (findings 1, 6, 8)

## What worked well

- The desk card's three answers (Why you / What you decide / What happens next) and Linda's limit bar explain one order better than any paragraph; keep them exactly as they are.
- The spreadsheet wizard is the clearest flow in the app: Kevin's nine row-by-row questions, "Yes, do that / Hold for review", then "Posted 16 lots as opening stock. 2 rows skipped; held for review: Xanthan Gum."
- Lots & recall earns its place: the recall drill ("Traced in 0.60 ms · by hand: about 2 days of paper", five customers, value at risk) and "Prove it" ("88 of 88 records proved… checked in 1.4 s", with "Change one record" to see a failure).
- Morning review drafts read as Says / Kevin / Signing does / Sign, and the signature shows up where it should: "BR-B-1049 moved from Nov 19 to Nov 12" and the Schedule page agrees.
- The 5am brief, Schedule, Group purchasing ("Who paid for it") and Reconciliation (7/7) pages are plain memos; once the twin draws, full screen with its tabs is good. No sideways scroll anywhere on the phone, and the dark Overview is readable.
