## The two-second idea

The flat map is a diagram: it shows *where things are* at a step. What it cannot show is the thing Day One is really about, which is **one purchase order moving through rules that nobody on the plant floor can see**. A judge who watches the 3D view for ten seconds should see a gold coin leave Acme HQ, bounce off an invisible wall at the escrow vault ("$5,150 is over Kevin's $2,500 limit"), get through after Linda's text, sit locked in the vault while a truck drives from the supplier to the dock, wait while the pallet sits in an amber cage, and fly from the vault to the supplier the second QC releases the lot. That motion *is* the product: the rule lives on the chain, the money waits on QC, and Kevin never touches a wallet. So the 3D view is built around motion between steps, not prettier buildings.

## What was built

Files are listed in §3; everything under `web/src/twin3d/`.

- **One state, two views.** `Twin3D` takes the same `TwinState` the flat map takes. Buildings are the boxes from `layout.ts` placed at `(x, 0, y)`; the pads and the arcs use the same anchors. Nothing is positioned by hand in the 3D code.
- **Transitions on the demo clock.** The view derives the state at the previous step and the current step and interpolates between them with an eased `u` from a clock that is either wall time scaled (live) or a fixed 1000/60 ms per frame (`?record=1`). No `setTimeout`, no `Math.random()`.
- **Shots fitted to the stage (Oct 29 polish).** A shot is a list of subjects (the corners of the floors and pads it shows, the points its labels hang from with the labels' size in pixels) and an angle; `frameShot` in `scene.ts` puts the target in the middle of them and backs the camera off until every subject, label included, sits inside the canvas with a 14 px margin. So the same shot stands farther back on a 4:3 phone stage than in the 16:9 wide view, and the story camera refits when the stage changes size. On a phone (560 px and under) the labels are left out of the fit, or fitting 160 px labels into a 330 px stage would shrink the plants to specks: there the labels are smaller, plant names only, and fade at the edge instead. `scene.test.ts` projects every step's subjects at both sizes and checks that none crosses the frame.
- **Labels that know where they are.** Labels are DOM (`drei/Html`), so no font file is needed and the page's own typeface is used, but a label's size is in pixels while its anchor is in the world. `Label` in `Twin3D.tsx` projects its anchor each frame and fades the label out when any of it would cross the frame edge (half a plant name at the edge of the stage reads as a mistake). Labels on coins held in the vault, and the three quote labels on the suppliers' pad, stack by whole pixels (`stackPx`), so they stay apart at every camera distance. A department pill behind a nearer one it would overprint hides for that frame. Department labels also hide when the camera is far and at phone width, like the flat map's.
- **Draw calls.** Every building part kind (bodies, gable and sawtooth roofs, silos, tanks, stacks, windows, doors, vents, roof units, docks) is one instanced mesh; the rest are a handful of meshes and lines: 37 to 66 draw calls over the run (`npm run web:shot:3d` prints them).
- **Bundle.** The default page's JavaScript is 294.7 KB (289.9 KB before this branch: the switcher and its styles); the 3D chunk is 980.7 KB (262.8 KB gzipped) and loads only on `?view=3d` or the switch.
- **Checked Oct 29** with `npm run web:shot:3d`: no console errors at any moment, light and dark, 1280 and 390 px; `RECORD=1` shows every clock sample a whole number of 1000/60 ms frames and the run reaching step 15 of 15 on its own at 42.07 s; a drag orbits the camera and the next step hands it back to the story framing. Headless Edge renders about 166 frames per second uncapped; the 60 fps claim at 1080p still needs a look on Tony's screen. **Oct 29 polish (unattended run):** the script now lists every visible label that crosses the stage edge or overprints another and exits 1 when one does; after the fitted shots and the fading, stacking labels it reports `label problems: 0` at every key moment, wide and phone (before: plant names cut at the edge in the money and quote shots, the vault half out of frame with its two PO labels overprinting at delivery, and labels cut at both edges on the phone).
- **Phone and no-WebGL fallback.** Under 560 px the labels thin out (names only, smaller), the shots fit the buildings rather than the labels, and the canvas keeps a 4:3 box; without WebGL the pane shows the flat map.

## Model changes outside `web/src/twin3d/`

- `web/src/twin/model.ts`: a new optional `quotes` array on `TwinState` (supplier, price, standing: selected / runner-up / excluded, reason, chainTx) filled at the Quotes step, and `formatQuotes(state)` for the 3D caption. The 2D map does not read either; its tests and drawing are unchanged. Tests added in `model.test.ts`.
- `web/src/App.tsx`: the map pane is `PlantView` (in `twin3d/`), which renders the flat map or lazy-loads the 3D view. Reverting is two lines if the 3D view is deleted.
- `tools/render/src/shot.ts`: `VIEW=3d` screenshots the full page with the 3D view. `tools/render/src/shot3d.ts` (`npm run web:shot:3d`, in `package.json` and `AGENTS.md`) captures the stage at the storyboard moments and, with `RECORD=1`, checks record mode.

## The group run (Oct 29, unattended run)

The map pane's bar has a second switch, **This run | Group buy**. "Group buy" plays the recorded group purchase (`web/src/data/group-buy.json`, docs/GROUP_BUY.md) on the same map, flat or 3D, with its own ‹ › Play controls and a note that it is the recorded purchase, not this run; `?run=group` opens on it. Nothing new was positioned by hand:

- **The model reads a pooled PO.** `deriveTwinState` matches `PO-G-2001` as well as `PO-1042`. The "Allocation:" line (or the funding call's "Plant B 4,745.00 DTUSD" parts) gives the shares; every money step then makes **one coin per plant share**, leaving from that plant's purchasing roof instead of HQ, with `share: { plant, total, approvedBy }` on the flow: both coins hit the vault's wall at the refusal, each director's YES lands as a note on their own plant and on their share, the retry puts both coins in the vault as one $9,490.00 escrow, the release pays once. The hub's receipt line "700 kg is earmarked for Plant A" becomes a truck from Plant B's warehouse to Plant A's on release (the inter-plant road idea 8 wanted). The "Pool" step leaves a note on each plant in the pool and on Plant C, which stayed out. `TwinOptions.plantB: "online"` starts every plant online, since the group run happens after onboarding, and `TwinState.pooled` names the pooled PO once it exists (the summary reads "PO-G-2001 pooled for $9,490.00: Plant B $4,745.00, Plant A $4,745.00.").
- **Shots.** `shotFor(title, pooled)`: the money steps of a pooled run frame both plants' purchasing roofs and the vault; the release frames the vault, the depots, Plant B and the truck's road to Plant A. The demo run's shots are unchanged (`scene.test.ts` checks both runs fit, wide and on a phone).
- **Labels that collide hide by rank.** Every DOM label reports its screen box each frame and one pass (`LabelPass`) keeps labels in rank order, coins over lots over plant names over pads, depots and department pills, nearer first among equals; a label that would overprint one already kept fades out until the camera moves them apart. This replaced the pill-only check and fixed the pooled run's collisions (two share coins at the wall also stack by whole pixels, like coins in the vault). A fiber gotcha found on the way: a `useFrame` with a positive priority switches off the renderer's own frame loop, so the pass runs at the default priority and a label mounted after it reports a frame late.
- **Checked** with `RUN=group npm run web:shot:3d` (12 moments, wide light and phone dark, curtain on for the money steps: 41–68 draw calls, no console errors, 0 label problems) and `RUN=group RECORD=1` (the group run reaches 12 of 12 on its own at 33.0 s); the demo run's `npm run web:shot:3d` still reports 0 label problems.

## The design pass (Oct 29, branch `ui-design`)

The twin's geometry and motion are unchanged. Its colours and DOM labels now read the page's tokens (`web/src/tokens.css`: `--brand`, `--refused`, `--quarantine`, `--money`, `--paid`, `--held`), so a quarantined lot, a refused coin and a paid coin wear the same pill colours as the cards on the Floor. The bullet list under the stage is gone from both the flat map and the twin: the map panel (`PlantView`) shows one caption line (the plants' state plus the newest money or material line) and a legend, and the full description stays as the canvas's accessible label. `npm run web:shot:3d` still reports 0 label problems at every moment, wide and phone, in both runs.

## The site plan (Oct 30, branch `v2-design`; docs/VISION.md §6.5, §6.12.1)

Tony asked for real buildings instead of a grid of boxes. Each plant is now a site (`web/src/twin/layout.ts`): Hollow Pine has a warehouse with its receiving docks and apron, the processing hall under a sawtooth roof (silos over the weigh cells, a blend tank, a dust stack over the presses), the dry side, the QC lab, a three-floor office with parking, a utilities yard, a gatehouse, a road and trees; Riverside is the same kit at seven tenths without a powder line; Lakeview is a warehouse, the dry side and an office. Each building is split into the ledger's twenty areas, and each area lights with the story department it belongs to (the nine `TwinState` tracks), on its roof panel or sawtooth glass and in its windows. The buildings stand from the start, since the plant existed before Day One; nothing rises any more. Flows land on the roof over each department's main area, and the quarantined pallet waits over the warehouse's quarantine area.

- **One instanced mesh per geometry** (boxes, cylinders, cones, gables, sawtooth, windows) instead of one per part kind: the busiest step is 57 draw calls, was 70.
- **Click any area** of any plant, 3D or flat, and the Plant map page opens its sheet (`web/src/pages/MapPage.tsx`; `GET /api/areas`, `server/src/ledger/areas.ts`): the equipment (`server/src/seed/equipment.ts`, fictional), the lots there now and the latest events, each event placed in an area by what it is, since lot events carry no area.
- **The stage's controls**: zoom in, zoom out and Fit (back to the story's framing), and layer switches for equipment, orders and money, and labels. A ring pings where each step's change happened.
- **Not built**: frosted-glass transmission (a second render pass per frame; the panes are opaque with the status colour), clicking the suppliers', escrow and HQ pads (their pages hold the same), and the rooms image Tony mentioned (not received; the hall keeps its sawtooth bays).

`npm run web:shot:3d` reports 0 label problems at every moment, wide and phone, in both runs.
